# The infinite AI feedback loop

**Pattern 013 · Category: automation**

**Created: 2026-10-25T20:54:12Z**

> *“Every problem has a solution. Every solution has a new problem.”*

## Problem

You have a simple development task that should take thirty minutes. Fortunately, generative AI can complete it in seconds. Doing it yourself would be a waste of time.

## Solution

Ask the AI to generate the code.

The result almost works. Share the error message, and the AI immediately suggests a fix. The fix introduces a regression, but another prompt resolves it.

The new implementation breaks the tests. Ask the AI to rewrite them. The updated tests reveal architectural issues, so the AI recommends a complete refactoring.

Follow its recommendations. Each improvement introduces new problems, which the AI confidently solves with further improvements. After several hours, your simple task has become a complete architectural overhaul. Fortunately, the AI is always available to help.

## Benefits

- Every problem receives an immediate solution.
- You never waste time investigating errors yourself.
- Your architecture continuously evolves.
- Every regression creates another opportunity for improvement.

## Success metric

Every AI-generated problem is successfully resolved by another AI-generated solution.

## The ultimate achievement

After hours of improvements, you finally ask the AI to review the result.

It replies: *“This implementation has several issues. I recommend starting from scratch.”*
