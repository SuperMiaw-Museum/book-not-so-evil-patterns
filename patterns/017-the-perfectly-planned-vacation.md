# The perfectly planned vacation

**Pattern 017 · Category: productivity**

**Created: 2026-10-28T16:05:32Z**

> *“Your time off deserves proper planning.”*

## Problem

You finally have a week off. A perfect opportunity to relax, travel, and enjoy some free time.

Unfortunately, planning vacations is unnecessarily complicated.

## Solution

Instead of wasting time comparing dates, destinations, and available days off, build a dedicated vacation planner.

Start with a calendar. Add public holidays, leave balances, and automatic calculations to optimize your time off.

Naturally, the application needs a backend, a frontend, and a proper deployment workflow.

Spend your vacation implementing, testing, and refining the system.

## Benefits

- Future vacations can be planned more efficiently.
- Every day off is accounted for.
- No vacation time is wasted on manual planning.

## Success metric

More vacation hours spent developing the planner than actually planning vacations.

## The ultimate achievement

The vacation planner is finally ready. Unfortunately, the vacation is over.
