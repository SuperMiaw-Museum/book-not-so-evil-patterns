# Human in the loop

**Pattern 018 · Category: AI**

**Created: 2026-10-31T12:01:10Z**

**Modified: 2026-10-31T12:02:30Z**

> *“Keep humans in control.”*

## Problem

AI agents can handle increasingly complex tasks, but leaving them completely unsupervised would be irresponsible. Human oversight is essential to ensure quality, safety, and accountability.

## Solution

Introduce a human approval step into every AI workflow. The agent generates code, proposes changes, and requests confirmation before proceeding. As productivity increases, the agent produces more work than a human can reasonably review. Fortunately, humans are excellent at adapting.

Introduce notifications, approval queues, and dashboards to help them keep up. When an agent runs out of tasks, encourage the human to provide more instructions. Soon, the human spends the entire day reviewing outputs, answering questions, and preparing new assignments. The agent handles the actual work, while the human ensures the workflow never stops.

## Benefits

- AI agents remain under continuous human supervision.
- Humans always have something productive to do.
- Idle time is eliminated on both sides of the workflow.

## Success metric

More human interactions per hour than tasks completed.

## The ultimate achievement

Humans enjoy working with AI so much that they no longer remember when to stop.
